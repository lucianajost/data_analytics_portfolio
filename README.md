### 🚕 Zuber Ride Analysis in Chicago
Exploratory data analysis and statistical hypothesis testing on ride-sharing data for Zuber, a fictional new ride-sharing company launching in Chicago. The project identifies the busiest taxi companies and destination neighborhoods, then tests whether weather conditions affect ride duration on trips between the Loop and O'Hare International Airport.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · SciPy · SQL

**Key topics:** Exploratory data analysis · Data visualization · Two-sample t-test · Hypothesis testing

**Key finding:** Rides between the Loop and O'Hare take significantly longer on rainy Saturdays than on clear ones (p ≈ 9.13 × 10⁻⁸), rejecting the null hypothesis that weather has no effect on ride duration.

> 📓 [View Notebook](zuber_chicago_ride_analysis.ipynb) · 📊 [View Presentation](Zuber_Chicago_Analysis_LuJ_Portfolio.pdf)

### 🎟️ Y.Afisha Business Analysis
Marketing efficiency and unit economics analysis for Y.Afisha, an entertainment ticketing platform, covering user behavior, cohort retention, and the relationship between acquisition cost and customer lifetime value.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn

**Key topics:** Cohort retention analysis · Conversion funnel · Customer acquisition cost (CAC) · Customer lifetime value (LTV) · ROMI

**Key finding:** No cohort fully recovered its acquisition cost within the observed window; average cumulative ROMI reached only 0.67 by month 11, and the median customer placed just one order, pointing to retention and repeat purchase as bigger levers than acquisition volume alone.

### ☕ LA Robot-Service Cafe Market Analysis
Market viability analysis for a robot-service cafe concept in Los Angeles, covering establishment types, chain versus independent structure, seat capacity benchmarks, and street-level location density across 9,650 restaurants.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Seaborn · Matplotlib

**Key topics:** Market segmentation · Chain structure analysis · Seat capacity benchmarking · Geographic density

**Key finding:** Cafes are the second most chain-dominant establishment type in the market (61.1%, after bakeries), and networks in the sample consistently favor compact units (25 to 30 seats) over large flagship locations, pointing to a repeatable, moderate-footprint format as the strongest entry strategy.

### 🧪 A/B Test Analysis & Hypothesis Prioritization
Business decision analysis for an online store, combining ICE/RICE prioritization of nine marketing hypotheses with a full statistical evaluation of an in-progress A/B test.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · SciPy · Matplotlib

**Key topics:** ICE and RICE scoring · A/B test data validation · Mann-Whitney U testing · Outlier-robust analysis

**Key finding:** After removing 58 users with cross-group contamination, group B showed a statistically significant conversion advantage over group A both in raw data (p ≈ 0.011) and after outlier removal (p ≈ 0.016), with no significant difference in average order value, supporting a decision to stop the test with group B as the winner.
